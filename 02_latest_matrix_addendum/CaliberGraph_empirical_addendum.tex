\head{Localized diagnosis, not a new method}
For toxicology pair 223, the queries return one and two columns. Polygon's saved database satisfies full C but produces two empty row sets in SQLite. The preregistered row-bag contract therefore does not accept it as distinguishing. The pinned runtime and upstream unequal-arity equality branch return ``either output is nonempty''; the calling context negates that condition. Saved SMT consequently requires both outputs empty. Independent review verified this local direction issue. No fix, new SQL, solver rerun, defect prevalence estimate or novelty claim is made here.

\head{What the new evidence establishes}
A preregistered small audit now has actual end-to-end records, complete denominators and independent witness replay. ParSEval's 18 distinguishing witnesses span all 11 schemas. Polygon's existing path gives one distinguishing witness and a localized non-distinguishing candidate with source-code and saved-formula support. These are concrete additions to the evidence, not promises of later experiments.

\head{Why the paper remains on hold}
The audit does not yet establish a transferable SQL-specific reliability finding. Witness generation across 11 schemas is not 11 replications of the same failure mechanism. Only three valid pairs enter Polygon's symbolic path. Different row bounds, schema adapters, semantic gates and generation strategies prevent matched cross-tool inference. Eight sampled agreements do not prove equivalence; one bounded UNSAT does not prove unbounded SQLite equivalence. All material is development-exposed, with no independent final validation set.

The original selector mechanism still has no demonstrated advantage over flat or cheap conjunction normalization. Reframing the work as an empirical paper avoids no evidence obligation, but an empirical paper does not inherently require a new algorithm.

\head{The missing systematic finding}
Evidence capable of changing this judgment would identify a prespecified SQL semantic obligation and a repeatable relationship between that obligation and observed declarations or witnesses across several schemas and genuinely comparable implementation domains. It would separate invalid inputs, frontend refusal, encoding or decoding mismatch, search budget and output-comparison contracts, and use targeted falsification to rule out confounding explanations.

A well-supported negative result could also qualify: a useful, explicitly scoped boundary where a proposed risk does not arise, with transparent denominators and uncertainty. Either direction needs known-defect and prior-work checks, clear tool lineage and versions, and separately reserved validation material not repeatedly used for development. These are evidence requirements, not authorization to launch a new campaign.

\head{No requirement to defeat every baseline}
Scientific value may lie in explaining when existing methods suffice, when differences disappear, or where a reproducible semantic boundary occurs. A method need not win against every baseline. The missing contribution is reusable new knowledge beyond additional logs, one engineering repair or adapter-dependent coverage. Contracts, checker design, hashing and refusal accounting already overlap P19 and cannot simply be reported again as new contributions. This addendum does not complete an exhaustive novelty or overlap review.
